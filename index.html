A Calm, Honest Guide to Crystals

Real Crystals, Real Stories, Real Life

Crystals, as most people use the word, are the polished stones and mineral specimens people collect, wear and keep close, for their beauty and for the meanings attached to them. Here you find what each stone really is, where its meaning comes from and one small ritual that fits a normal day.

    The Stone Atlas

    Every stone by name and alias, with what it is, what people believe about it and how to care for it. Type the name on a label and the Atlas tells you what it usually means. Not every "crystal" is one, by the way. Obsidian is volcanic glass that cooled too fast for crystals to form, and amber is fossilized tree resin.

    Before a Hard Conversation

    Keep the stone in your pocket on the day of the talk. Before you walk in, hold it and decide two things, the one sentence you need the other person to hear and the one thing you will listen for. Put it back in your pocket and go.
