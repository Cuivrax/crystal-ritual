# Virgo Crystals & Birthstones, Plus Where the Links Come From

Virgo has no single official birthstone, because birthstones belong to months and Virgo runs from about August 23 to September 22. That gives peridot, sardonyx or spinel for an August birthday and sapphire for a September one. As a zodiac stone, a 1913 list gives Virgo the carnelian, while today's crystal guides favor purpurite, moss agate and diopside, all beliefs rather than mineral facts.

## What Is the Virgo Birthstone?

The honest answer has two parts, the month stone and the sign stone, and for Virgo the old sign stone has an unusually long record.

**The month stone.** On the modern list of the [American Gem Society](https://www.americangemsociety.org/birthstones/), August has three birthstones, peridot, sardonyx and spinel, and September has one, sapphire. The 1912 jewelers' list gave August the sardonyx with peridot as its alternate and September the sapphire. Kunz objected that chrysolite, his name for peridot, had always been the September gem, and that sapphire was "properly the gem for April" (1913, pages 319 to 320). Spinel joined August in July 2016. Our [August](/birthstones/august/) and [September](/birthstones/september/) pages go stone by stone.

**The sign stone.** **Tradition.** The traditional Virgo zodiac stone is the carnelian, from the verse list of zodiac gems that George Frederick Kunz printed in 1913. "Success will bless whate'er you do, / Through Virgo's sign, if only you / Place on your hand her own gem true, / Carnelian" (*The Curious Lore of Precious Stones*, page 346). An old Spanish list that Kunz thought probably reflected Arab tradition gives Virgo the "magnet" instead (page 347), most likely the lodestone, since Kunz elsewhere writes of "a magnet, or loadstone" (page 134).

Carnelian also led August in the old month lists Kunz compared (page 316), and in his own calendar of the months August carries the carnelian, the sign of Virgo and the poppy, with a verse that begins "Wear a carnelian or for thee / No conjugal felicity" (page 329). Carnelian is not on today's month list, but for Virgo it has the oldest claim.

### What Color Is the Virgo Birthstone?

There is no single Virgo birthstone color. Peridot is mostly yellowish green, sardonyx is banded red-brown and white, and spinel comes in red, pink, orange, purple, blue and black. Sapphire is best known in blue, but gem corundum of every color except red counts as sapphire. The 1913 carnelian runs from yellow-orange to reddish orange to orangey brown.

Modern guides pull toward green, with moss agate and diopside, and toward purple, with purpurite at the top. So the old answer for Virgo's color is carnelian orange, and the modern one is green.

## Virgo Crystals in Today's Guides

**Modern Association.** Most lists of crystals for Virgo come from modern crystal guides, which describe Virgo as analytical, hardworking and often self-critical, and many of their picks are meant to soften that. One guide says moss agate brings "these typically analytical minds some much-appreciated calmness," and another offers purpurite to "any overworked Virgo." One French guide calls Virgo an earth sign ruled by Mercury.

### Purpurite

**Classic.** [Purpurite](/stones/purpurite/) is a rare manganese iron phosphate, reddish purple to deep rose-red, and its name comes from the Latin for purple. Alteration often darkens its surface to brown or black, and specimens may be lightly treated with oxalic acid to brighten the purple. Most gem material comes from Usakos, Namibia. At 4 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale it is too soft for jewelry wear, so this Classic stone is really a shelf stone.

### Moss Agate

**Widely Linked.** [Moss agate](/stones/moss-agate/) is chalcedony with plant-like patterns, made by inclusions that only look like vegetation. It rates 6.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale, and notable sources include India, Scotland and the northwestern United States.

### Diopside

**Widely Linked.** [Diopside](/stones/diopside/) is a pyroxene mineral, usually light to dark green. Chrome diopside owes its deep green to chromium, and Siberia supplies most of it for jewelry. It rates 5.5 to 6.5 with perfect cleavage, so it suits earrings and pendants better than rings. Diopside gems are generally not treated.

### Carnelian

**Widely Linked.** [Carnelian](/stones/carnelian/) is a translucent orange to red to brown chalcedony colored by iron, 7 on the Mohs scale, and most commercial carnelian comes from India. Heat treatment darkens its red, so assume a piece has been enhanced unless told otherwise, and much material sold as carnelian is plainer agate that was heated or stained. One guide still calls carnelian "the birthstone for the zodiac sign of Virgo," a rare case where a modern guide repeats the 1913 list.

### Andalusite

**Widely Linked.** [Andalusite](/stones/andalusite/) is an aluminum silicate, sharing its chemistry with kyanite and sillimanite. It shows different colors from different angles, an effect called pleochroism, and it rates 6.5 to 7.5 on the Mohs scale. Brazil is the main gem source. It is brittle with distinct cleavage, so ring stones need protective settings.

### Sapphire for September Virgos

**Sometimes Linked.** [Sapphire](/stones/sapphire/) is corundum, 9 on the Mohs scale and superb for everyday wear. Many treatment methods exist, and milky Sri Lankan stones have been heated for decades to turn them blue. One guide calls sapphire "above all a stone of Virgo," though most guides leave it off their Virgo lists.

### August Stones for August Virgos

**Rarely Linked.** Peridot and sardonyx turn up in only a few Virgo lists, and spinel in almost none. All three are covered on the [Leo](/zodiac/leo/) page, the sign that shares August.

## Where the Virgo Links Come From

**The Story.** Virgo's oldest links run almost entirely through one red stone.

- **Roman Empire.** **Tradition.** Roman engraved gems often carried the wearer's birth sign, usually on onyx, carnelian and similar stones (Kunz 1913, pages 124 to 125). A carnelian once in the Cabinet du Roi in Paris showed Jupiter enthroned inside a border of the twelve signs, with Virgo as a virgin and a unicorn, a type said to have been used only under Domitian, 81 to 96 CE (page 341).
- **Medieval Jewish tradition.** **Tradition.** The tribe of Gad was paired with Virgo (Kunz 1913, page 314).
- **13th century.** **Tradition.** The Lapidario of Alfonso X of Castile, a Spanish compilation built on a much older original (Kunz 1913, page 14), recommended carnelian to people with a weak voice or who were timid in speech, so that they would "speak both boldly and well" (page 63). The same work gave the carnelian its virtue from the Sun and Venus (page 348). Another old list puts it under Jupiter, Mars and Venus (page 351).
- **1913.** **Tradition.** Kunz's verse list names the carnelian (page 346), and his calendar gives August the carnelian and the sign of Virgo (page 329). The old Spanish list names the magnet (page 347).
- **Today.** **Modern Association.** Purpurite, moss agate, diopside, andalusite and carnelian lead the modern lists of zodiac stones for Virgo.

Carnelian is the rare thread that runs unbroken. The 1913 verse, Kunz's month calendar and today's guides all point to it.

## How to Use Virgo Stones in Everyday Life

**The Ritual.** Virgo season is back-to-school and back-to-work season. Whatever you believe about the stones, these give a pause you chose and something in your hand to mark it.

### A Carnelian for the Call You Keep Postponing

**Try This.** Keep a tumbled carnelian next to your phone. Before the call or the meeting you have been putting off, hold the stone and say your first sentence out loud once, then dial. The Lapidario of Alfonso X recommended carnelian to timid speakers. The rehearsal is the part you can count on.

### A Done List Instead of a To-Do List

**Try This.** At the end of the workday, write three things you actually finished and set a moss agate on the page. Guides call Virgo analytical and overworked, and a done list shows the work already behind you.

### A Birthday Card with Two Stones

**Try This.** For an August Virgo, give a peridot or a sardonyx and copy the 1913 carnelian verse inside the card. For a September Virgo, give a sapphire and add one line about the Roman carnelian with its unicorn Virgo.

**Safety.** Small tumbled stones and bead bracelets are not toys. Keep them away from small children and pets who might swallow them. These are personal practices, not treatments.
