# Real or Fake, Know What You Are Buying

Most crystals sold as fake are not fake at all. They are real stones that were heated, dyed or given a prettier name. A smaller share are true imitations, glass or resin sold as something else. These guides show you which is which, with checks you can do at home without harming the stone.

## Decode the Label

Shop names sell, mineral names tell you what you hold. The [Crystal Label Decoder](/tools/crystal-label-decoder/) translates the name on a tag into what the stone really is and whether it is natural, treated or an imitation.

## Stones People Mix Up

Some pairs fool almost everyone. See [Garnet vs Ruby](/compare/garnet-vs-ruby/) and [Opal vs Opalite](/compare/opal-vs-opalite/) for the differences that matter.

## Treated Is Not Fake

Heating and dyeing are old and common practices. A treated stone is still a real stone, it simply has to be sold as treated. An imitation is a different material sold under a stone's name. That line is the one worth learning.
