# Unicorn Jasper Meaning, Properties & What It Really Is

Unicorn jasper is a seller's name with no published definition. We found no gem lab, museum or mineral reference that describes it, so what the tag covers depends on the shop. If the stone is true jasper, it is an opaque, fine-grained quartz rock rated 7 on the Mohs hardness scale, which runs from 1 (softest) to 10 (hardest). Any meaning attached to the name is modern.

Glittery pink or purple flakes point to the mica-rich mix sold as unicorn stone, not to jasper. True jasper is opaque, so a thin edge should stay dark or nearly so under a flashlight. ## What Unicorn Jasper Actually Is

**The Stone.** Jasper is an opaque, solid or patterned variety of cryptocrystalline quartz, colored by mineral impurities. Its names often come from a color, a pattern or a place, like bloodstone, orbicular or Morrisonite. "Unicorn" is none of those, so the name tells you nothing about the rock. Our [jasper page](/stones/jasper/) covers the real thing.

One crystal dealer counts hundreds of jasper trade names, many offering "little or no clue" about where a stone comes from or what it is like, and notes that "jasper" is often used for opaque colored stones, many of them not true jaspers in the geological sense. So even the word jasper on the tag is not a guarantee.

### Unicorn Jasper or Unicorn Stone?

The closest documented name is unicorn stone, which is not jasper at all. One crystal seller describes a recent find from Minas Gerais of quartz, lepidolite and feldspar with pink, green, purple and blue tourmaline, a combination "sometimes known as 'Unicorn Stone.'"

That mix makes geological sense. [Lepidolite](/stones/lepidolite/) is a lithium-rich mica, usually pink, red or purple, that grows in pegmatites, rocks that crystallize late from a cooling magma, alongside tourmaline, quartz and feldspar. Minas Gerais is one of its notable sources. A "unicorn" piece that glitters is more likely this mix than jasper.

## Is My Unicorn Jasper Real?

We use the same five labels on every stone.

- **Natural, untreated.** Possible, if the seller can name the rock and where it was mined.
- **Natural, treated.** Dyeing is common and stable in jasper, and heating, which changes its color, is common, stable and undetectable.
- **Lab-grown.** None documented.
- **Imitation.** None documented, though with no standard material, a mislabeled stone is the likelier problem.
- **Trade name.** Unicorn jasper and unicorn stone are both trade names.

Three checks you can do at home, with their limits.

1. **Look for glitter.** Under a 10x loupe, lepidolite shows stacks of thin, flat sheets with a pearly to glassy shine. Jasper has a waxy to dull luster and no flakes. This tells you which family you have, not where it came from.
2. **Shine a light through an edge.** In a dim room, press a phone flashlight to a thin edge. Jasper is opaque, so an edge that glows brightly is probably not textbook jasper.
3. **Ask about dye.** A gemologist detects dye by its appearance, a spectroscope or a Chelsea filter, a small color filter used in gem testing. Ask the seller and get the answer in writing.

Three questions for the seller. What mineral or rock is this? Where was it mined? Has it been dyed or heated? The [Label Decoder](/tools/crystal-label-decoder/) does not list unicorn jasper yet, but it explains other words you may see on the same tag, such as genuine or AAA grade.

## How to Care for Unicorn Jasper

**Mineral Fact.** True jasper is trouble free to care for. Since the name does not tell you the material, and jasper is often dyed, the care below is the cautious choice.

- **Cleaning.** Wipe it with a soft cloth dampened in lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap, then dry it at once. One antiques reference lists warm soapy water as safe for true jasper.
- **Water.** No Soaking. A long soak is not covered, even for true jasper.
- **Heat.** Keep Away From Heat. Heat may change jasper's color, and dyed pieces should stay cool.
- **Chemicals.** No Chemicals. Dyed pieces should avoid harsh chemicals, so skip bleach and household cleaners.
- **Salt.** Not Enough Data. Leave it out.
- **Storage.** Handle Gently if it glitters. Lepidolite rates only 2.5 to 3.5 and splits easily along its sheets, so give a mica-rich piece its own pouch.

**Safety.** Small tumbled stones and bead bracelets are not toys. Keep them away from small children and pets who might swallow them.

## Price Guide

Jasper in general is very common, so most of a piece's value comes from the strength of its color, the beauty of its pattern and the skill of the cutting or carving. A few named jaspers, such as imperial jasper, cost more because they are relatively rare, but unicorn jasper is not a documented type. Ask what the stone is before paying extra for the name.
