## Five Minutes, Right When You Need Them

Pick the moment you are in. Each ritual takes five minutes and one stone, any stone you like.

### Five Minutes After a Long Day

Evening. Phone away, lamp low, a stone in your hand and five slow breaths before dinner. [Crystals for grounding](/crystals-for/grounding/)

### Five Minutes Before a Hard Talk

Daytime. Hold the stone and decide two things, the one sentence they need to hear and the one thing you will listen for. [From the pink tourmaline page](/stones/pink-tourmaline/)

### Five Minutes During a Bath

Self-Care. The stone waits on the edge of the tub, never in the water. Ten slow breaths while the bath runs. [Which crystals can go in water](/care/crystals-that-cant-go-in-water/)

### Five Minutes at Your Desk

Daytime. Before you answer the message that annoyed you, pick up the stone and let one slow breath out. [Crystals for focus](/crystals-for/focus/)

### Five Minutes Before Lights Out

Evening. Write one line about something you handled kindly today, then set the stone on the closed notebook. [Crystals for sleep](/crystals-for/sleep/)

### Five Minutes on Full Moon Night

Moon. Jar on the windowsill, stone beside it, one line on a card about what you are done carrying. [Moon water, the safe way](/rituals/moon-water/)

## The Stone Atlas

Every stone by name and alias, with what it is, what people believe about it and how to care for it. Type the name on a label and the Atlas tells you what it usually means. Not every "crystal" is one, by the way. Obsidian is volcanic glass that cooled too fast for crystals to form ([Geology.com](https://geology.com/rocks/obsidian.shtml)), and amber is fossilized tree resin ([GIA](https://www.gia.edu/amber/gem-overview)).

### Pink Tourmaline

A pink color of tourmaline, not a mineral species of its own ([IGS](https://www.gemsociety.org/article/tourmaline-jewelry-and-gemstone-information/)). Hardness 7 to 7.5. Love, Widely Linked. [Read the page](/stones/pink-tourmaline/)

### Amethyst

The purple color variety of quartz ([GIA](https://www.gia.edu/amethyst/gem-overview)). Hardness 7. Sleep, Classic. [Read the page](/stones/amethyst/)

### Moldavite

A natural glass thought to have formed in the heat of an asteroid impact ([Geology.com](https://geology.com/gemstones/moldavite/)). Hardness 5 to 5.5. Natural Glass. [Read the page](/stones/moldavite/)

### Jade

One name for two different minerals, jadeite and nephrite ([Geology.com](https://geology.com/gemstones/jade/)). Hardness 6 to 7. Money, Classic. [Read the page](/stones/jade/)
