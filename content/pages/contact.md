## What Helps Most

- **A correction.** The page address, what is wrong and, if you can, a link to a source. When we fix a factual error, we add a dated note at the end of the page.
- **A source we missed.** An old text, a museum record or a study that belongs on a page is always worth a message.
- **A question.** If a page left you with one, the next reader probably has it too.

## What We Cannot Do

We cannot identify a stone from a photo. Nobody can do that with certainty, and a guess could cost you money. Our guides, starting with [real citrine vs heated amethyst](/real-or-fake/citrine/), explain what you can check at home and when only a gem lab can answer.

We cannot give medical, veterinary or mental health advice. If you are worried about your health or a pet's, please talk to a doctor or a vet. In the US, if you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at 988, or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org/), any time, day or night.

How we write and correct pages is set out in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/), and who is behind the site is on the [about page](/about/).
