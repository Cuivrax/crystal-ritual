# Peach Moonstone Meaning, Properties & Real-Life Uses

Peach moonstone is moonstone with a warm peach body color, a feldspar whose soft glow seems to float and move just under the surface. Crystal guides link it with calm, gentle love and new beginnings. Those meanings are modern beliefs. Like all moonstone, it has two directions of perfect cleavage, so one hard knock can split it.

> **Is It Real? Quick Checks.** Tilt it under one lamp. Moonstone's glow floats just under the surface and moves as you turn it. Glitter means sunstone, no glow means plain feldspar or another stone, and a visible seam on the side can mean an assembled stone. A gemologist confirms the rest.

## What Peach Moonstone Actually Is

**The Stone.** Moonstone is the popular name for gem-quality feldspar that shows adularescence, a soft glow that appears to float just below the surface of a polished stone. The name comes from that glow, which resembles the moon through thin cloud. Feldspar minerals make up more than half of Earth's rocky crust, yet moonstone is rare in top quality.

Most moonstone is orthoclase, a potassium feldspar.

### What Makes the Glow?

As the feldspar cools, orthoclase and albite separate into thin alternating layers. Those layers are about 0.5 microns thick, similar to a wavelength of light, so light entering the stone scatters between them and seems to billow across it. When the albite layers are very fine, the sheen is blue. When they are thicker, it is white. The sheen is usually white to silvery, which is normal, not a defect.

### Why Is It Peach?

Moonstone body colors, from white and beige through orange, red-brown and yellowish, are influenced by iron content, and goethite, an iron oxide, adds red tones. No reference pins the peach shade on one cause. Peach is the body color, the color of the stone itself, while the sheen floating on top is a separate effect. A clear stone with an orange flash is not peach moonstone, and may be rainbow moonstone, whose flashes can include orange.

### Where Peach Moonstone Comes From

India is a source of peach moonstone, and Indian moonstones with strong body color are plentiful, well cut and affordable. One mineral dealer adds Sri Lanka and Madagascar.

### Is Peach Moonstone Real Moonstone?

Yes. The Gemological Institute of America lists peach among moonstone's colors, so peach moonstone is the same gem as white or gray moonstone with a warmer body color. "Rainbow moonstone," by contrast, is technically labradorite, a different feldspar, though the trade accepts the name. See [rainbow moonstone](/stones/rainbow-moonstone/), and our [moonstone page](/stones/moonstone/) for the whole family.

## Where the Meaning Comes From

**The Story.** Peach moonstone has no history of its own, so its older lore belongs to moonstone in general.

- **Ancient Rome.** One seller says Pliny named moonstone for its link with the moon. His *selenitis*, said to hold a figure that followed the moon, is read by his 1855 translators as selenite, a form of gypsum, not feldspar (*Natural History*, Book 37, chapter 67).
- **India.** **Tradition.** Kunz wrote that "the moonstone is believed to bring good fortune and is regarded as a sacred stone in India," never displayed for sale except on a yellow cloth. It ranked high as a lovers' gift, said to let a couple read their future if held in the mouth under a full moon (*The Curious Lore of Precious Stones*, 1913, pages 97 to 98).
- **The East.** **Tradition.** Moonstone, star sapphire and cat's-eye all carry a moving light, and Kunz reports the belief that "a living spirit dwells within these stones, a spirit potent for good" (1913, pages 7 to 8).
- **Early 1500s, Rome.** **Tradition.** Pope Leo X was said to own a stone that brightened as the moon waxed and dimmed as it waned. Kunz calls the tale one of the fables spread to boost a fine moonstone in the papal treasury (1915, pages 386 to 387).
- **Days of the week.** **Tradition.** An old print pairs Diana, the sign of Cancer and the moonstone with Monday, and in Siam white fabrics and moonstones were prescribed for Monday (1913, pages 332 and 335).
- **1912.** Moonstone was one of the original modern birthstones, for June. Kunz was unimpressed, writing that "for June we should prefer the asteria to the moonstone as a substitute for the pearl" (1913, page 320). In Germany and Scandinavia, moonstone is the preferred June stone.
- **Today.** **Modern Association.** Peach moonstone's own meanings are recent, built on its color and the moon's cycle.

## What People Use Peach Moonstone for Today

### What Does Peach Moonstone Mean?

**Modern Association.** In modern crystal guides, peach moonstone is a stone of calm, gentle love and new beginnings. It is Widely Linked with [anxiety](/crystals-for/anxiety/) and Often Linked with love, sleep and protection in our ranking. Guides most often place it on the sacral chakra, the center of emotion and creativity in chakra charts, and Cancer, the sign ruled by the Moon in astrology, is the sign named most.

Guides call peach's energy gentle and "feminine," and one shop casts it as the Maiden of a Triple Goddess. The moon's monthly cycle gives it the new beginnings theme, and the old Indian lovers' gift fits the love link.

Some guides go further, claiming it regulates the menstrual cycle, eases menopause, boosts fertility, or helps digestion, skin and sleep. Mineralogy and medicine do not support those claims.

## How to Use Peach Moonstone in Everyday Life

**The Ritual.** Moonstone's moving light makes a simple cue.

### A New Moon Fresh Start

**Try This.** On the night of the new moon, or on the first of the month, write one small thing you are starting on an index card, such as a walk after lunch. Read it aloud with the stone in your hand, then set the stone on the card where you will see it each morning. More in our [new moon ritual](/rituals/new-moon-ritual/).

### The Lamp-Off Minute

**Try This.** Keep a peach moonstone on your nightstand. Before you switch off the lamp, tilt the stone slowly until the glow slides across it. Watch it for three slow breaths, then put the stone down and turn the light off.

### A June Birthday Gift

**Try This.** For a June birthday, give a peach moonstone pendant or earrings rather than a ring, with a card. Write what it is (feldspar with a glow made by layers about as thin as a wavelength of light) and one line of history, that in India it was a lovers' gift and sold only on a yellow cloth.

**Safety.** Small tumbled stones and bead bracelets are not toys, so keep them away from small children and pets. One guide calls moonstone suitable for gemstone water, but our care data rates soaking Brief Only, so keep it out of anything you drink.

**What Research Says.** People are more likely to start goal-directed behavior right after a date that feels like a fresh start, such as a new week, month or year, according to three archival field studies of Google searches, gym visits and goal commitments ([Dai, Milkman and Riis, *Management Science*, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2014.1901)). The studies observed patterns rather than running an experiment, and none involved stones or moon phases.

> **When a Stone Is Not Enough.** Peach moonstone is often chosen for periods, menopause, fertility, pregnancy, puberty, anxiety and sleep. A stone can be a comfort to hold, but it is not care. Talk to a doctor or a midwife about cycles, fertility and pregnancy, to a pediatrician about a teenager's health, and to a doctor or therapist when anxiety or poor sleep lasts. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988, or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org/), any time, day or night, in the United States. In an emergency, call 911.

## Is My Peach Moonstone Real?

We use the same five labels on every stone.

- **Natural, untreated.** The normal case.
- **Natural, treated.** No treatment is documented for peach moonstone.
- **Lab-grown.** Lab-created moonstones have not entered the market, though they may exist for research.
- **Imitation.** Milky chalcedony and some synthetic spinel have imitated moonstone, though they usually look inferior. Crystal sellers warn that "opalite," a man-made glass, is sold as moonstone. GIA has examined assembled cabochons of quartz, an iridescent coating and gypsum made to look like rainbow moonstone, and in 1915 Kunz described US beach quartz pebbles cut and sold as moonstones.
- **Trade names.** "Peach moonstone" is an honest descriptive name. Moonstone is also called adularia, after Mt. Adular in Switzerland.

### Peach Moonstone, Sunstone or Plain Feldspar?

Most mix-ups are other feldspars, and the light tells them apart.

- **Glow that floats and moves.** Moonstone.
- **Glitter, like tiny mirrors.** [Sunstone](/stones/sunstone/). Flat, reflective plates of hematite, goethite or copper flash together at the right angle, an effect called aventurescence.
- **No glow at all.** A pink or peach feldspar without adularescence is feldspar, not moonstone. A soft orange stone with no sheen may be [orange calcite](/stones/orange-calcite/), which rates only 3 on the Mohs scale.
- **Blue with green or orange flashes.** Labradorite sold as rainbow moonstone.

Three checks you can do at home, with their limits.

1. **Tilt it under one light.** In a dim room, turn the stone slowly under a single lamp, then move the lamp or your head. Moonstone's glow moves each time, but doublets can look similar.
2. **Look for centipedes.** With a 10x loupe, look for short pairs of parallel cracks with tiny cracks branching off at right angles, like many-legged insects. They are characteristic of moonstone, but not every stone shows them.
3. **Check the side and back.** On a set or loose cabochon, look for a straight seam where two layers meet. In one lab case, gas bubbles in the cement layer gave the join away. No seam does not prove a solid stone.

A gemologist can confirm moonstone with a refractive index reading, a measure of how strongly it bends light, typically 1.520 to 1.525. Three questions for the seller. Is this moonstone, or another feldspar? Is it solid, or a doublet? Where was it mined? The [Label Decoder](/tools/crystal-label-decoder/) translates names like these.

## How to Care for Peach Moonstone

**Mineral Fact.** Moonstone rates 6 to 6.5 on the Mohs scale of 1 to 10, has poor toughness, meaning it chips and breaks easily, and two directions of perfect cleavage, planes of weakness where it splits cleanly. One impact against a hard object can cleave it in two, so makers usually set it in pendants, earrings and pins rather than rings.

- **Water.** OK. Warm, soapy water is the only cleaning substance GIA recommends. Soaking is Brief Only.
- **Sun.** OK. Moonstone is stable to light, so fading is not the worry. Heat is.
- **Heat.** Keep Away From Heat. High heat or a sudden temperature change can cause breaks.
- **Chemicals.** No Chemicals. Moonstone is harmed by hydrofluoric acid, and harsh cleaners have no place near it.
- **Salt.** Not Enough Data, so skip salt cleanses.
- **Cleaning.** OK by hand. No Ultrasonic or Steam, ever.
- **Knocks and storage.** Handle Gently. Store it in its own pouch, away from harder stones.
- **Toxicity.** OK. None known for moonstone. Dust from cutting or polishing is a separate matter.

### How to Clean a Peach Moonstone Ring or Pendant

1. **Fill a bowl with warm water.** Warm, never hot, with one drop of mild dish soap.
2. **Brush for a minute.** Use a soft toothbrush on the stone and around the setting.
3. **Rinse in the same temperature water.** Avoid swapping from warm to cold, since sudden temperature change can crack moonstone.
4. **Dry and store.** Pat dry with a soft cloth and put it away in its own pouch.

### Can Peach Moonstone Go in the Sun or Salt?

The sun, yes. One seller warns against long direct sun, but GIA says moonstone is stable to light, and our care data follows GIA. A sill that turns hot is another matter. Salt is Not Enough Data, and a few hours in spring water, as one guide suggests, goes beyond Brief Only. Check your piece in the [Care Checker](/tools/crystal-care-checker/).

## Price Guide

The highest moonstone prices go to clear, colorless stones with a vivid blue sheen. For a peach piece, compare these.

- **Translucency.** Translucent cabochons with a good body color and sheen are common and moderately priced. Near-opaque stones, often carved into "moon faces," are the budget option.
- **The glow.** Strong, even adularescence across the whole face, not just a patch.
- **Cut.** A high-domed cabochon cut with its base parallel to the plane of the sheen shows the glow at its best.
- **Solid or assembled.** Ask whether it is solid or a doublet, and get the answer on the receipt.
