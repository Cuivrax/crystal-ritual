# Crystal Care, Keep Your Stones Safe

Crystal care comes down to four things, water, sunlight, heat and scratches. Some stones shrug off all four. Others dissolve, fade, crack or scratch in ways no ritual can undo. The guides below sort stones by what they can take, so you check before you rinse, soak or leave one on a sunny sill.

## Check Your Own Stone

The fastest way is the [Crystal Care Checker](/tools/crystal-care-checker/). Pick your stone and it tells you whether water, sun, salt and heat are safe, with the reason for each answer.

## Cleaning Is Not Cleansing

Cleaning is physical, it removes dust and skin oils. Cleansing is a belief, a way of resetting a stone in crystal practice. We cover both, and we always put the physical safety of the stone first. A method that harms the mineral is never worth it.
