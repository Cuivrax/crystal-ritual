# Find Your Stone, Start With What You Need

Most people come to crystals with a need, not a mineral name. Each page below takes one need and counts which stones crystal guides name most often for it, so you can see where the agreement is strong and where it is thin. Beliefs are shared as beliefs. The mineral facts and the care notes are checked against gem references.

## How We Rank the Stones

For every need we read the same set of crystal guides and count how many of them name each stone. A stone named in 11 of 12 guides sits above one named in 7. The count tells you what the tradition says, it does not tell you a stone works. Then we add what the guides often skip, what the stone really is, how hard it is and what damages it.
