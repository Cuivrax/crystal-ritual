# Mango Quartz Meaning, Properties & What It Really Is

Mango quartz is a trade name for clear to white quartz from Boyacá, Colombia, whose tips glow yellow to orange. GIA's journal says the color is believed to be due to halloysite, a clay mineral. Crystal sellers link it to courage, positivity and new beginnings. The honest note is that even the cause of the color is not yet confirmed.

Color spread evenly through a whole crystal, or color that lives only in cracks, deserves a question. Ask for the locality, which should be Boyacá, Colombia. Only a gem lab can confirm halloysite.

## What Mango Quartz Actually Is

**The Stone.** Mango quartz is quartz with a fruit name. At the 2023 Tucson shows, *Gems & Gemology*, the journal of the Gemological Institute of America, pictured a spray of "mango" quartz about 3.6 by 4.1 inches (92 by 103 mm) from Boyacá, Colombia, and wrote that "the yellow coloration is believed to be due to the mineral halloysite." Sellers describe clear to white quartz points and clusters with yellow to orange tips, and one calls the clay inclusions the cause of the color.

The two minerals could hardly be more different.

- **Quartz** is silicon dioxide, rated 7 on the Mohs scale, which ranks scratch resistance from 1 to 10. It is chemically inert in contact with most substances.
- **Halloysite** is a clay mineral, Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄, the same formula as kaolinite in a different structure. Under a microscope it forms tiny tubes. It rates only 2 to 2.5, and it forms when minerals such as feldspar are altered by hot fluids or by weathering.

Pure halloysite is white, and its yellow, red and other colors come from impurities inside it, so the exact cause of the mango color is not settled.

### Where Mango Quartz Comes From

From Boyacá, Colombia. Sellers name the locality as Cabiche, in the municipality of Quípama, and one dealer says it occurs in only one place in the world. Shops also tell a legend, in versions that differ, that the crew of emerald mine owner Victor Carranza found and sealed the pocket in the late 1980s, and that a local farmer's family stumbled on it again in 2017.

## Where the Meaning Comes From

**The Story.** Mango quartz has no old lore, since sellers date its arrival to 2020. Its name comes from its color, and its other name, corona quartz, comes from the crown of yellow points, which one shop says symbolizes ascension. Its meanings come from a handful of shop pages, and they travel. One shop's lines about "love, light, and dedication" and taking responsibility for your own happiness reappear, lightly reworded, on another shop's product page.

## What People Use Mango Quartz for Today

**Modern Association.** Crystal sellers describe it as a stone for courage, positivity and resilience, for transformation and determination, and for letting go to make room for new opportunities. One shop says it purifies your thoughts so you can stay true to yourself, and another links it to all chakras. These are beliefs, and none of them has been tested.

## How to Use Mango Quartz in Everyday Life

**The Ritual.** Mango quartz usually comes as points and clusters, a stone for the shelf more than the pocket, and this use suits that.

### A Fresh Start Landmark

**Try This.** Pick a date that feels like a new start, January 1, a birthday or simply next Monday. That morning, set the crystal where you see it first and slip a card under it with one goal and the first small step. Each time the yellow tips catch your eye, take that step or write the next one.

**What Research Says.** In three archival field studies, Google searches for "diet," gym visits and commitments to pursue goals rose right after dates that feel like a fresh start, such as a new week, month or year, a birthday or a holiday (Dai, Milkman and Riis, *Management Science*, 2014, [doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2014.1901](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2014.1901)). The studies observed patterns rather than running an experiment, and nobody used a stone. Here the crystal only marks the date.

**Safety.** Small points and loose crystals are not toys. Keep them away from small children and pets who might swallow them.

## Is My Mango Quartz Real?

### Is Mango Quartz Natural?

Yes, as far as anyone has reported. Sellers offer it as natural and untreated, and we found no report of dyed, heated or coated mango quartz.

- **Natural, untreated.** The material GIA pictured and sellers describe.
- **Natural, treated.** None reported. Quartz in general can be dyed by heating it and quenching it in a dye bath, which leaves color in a web of fine cracks.
- **Lab-grown.** None known under this name.
- **Imitation.** We found no report of imitations sold as mango quartz.
- **Trade names.** Mango quartz, halloysite quartz and corona quartz all name the same Colombian material.

### Two Checks You Can Do at Home

You need a desk lamp and a 10x loupe.

1. **Look where the color sits.** Hold one point under the lamp and follow the color from base to tip. In the pieces sellers describe and photograph, the yellow to orange gathers toward the tips inside clear or white quartz. A crystal that is evenly orange from base to tip is worth a question.
2. **Look at the cracks with the loupe.** Dye collects in fractures and pits, darker there and lighter on smooth faces. Orange that traces a web of cracks fits dyed quartz, not mango quartz.

These checks can rule a piece out, but none can confirm halloysite. That takes a gem lab.

### What to Ask the Seller

1. Where exactly was it mined? The answer should be Boyacá, Colombia. Another origin means different quartz sold under the name.
2. Has the color been treated in any way? Ask for "natural, untreated" in writing on the receipt.

Under the US Federal Trade Commission's Jewelry Guides, a seller should disclose a treatment that is not permanent, that creates special care needs or that significantly affects value. The [Label Decoder](/tools/crystal-label-decoder/) turns the words on a tag into what they usually mean.

## How to Care for Mango Quartz

**Mineral Fact.** The host is quartz, hard and stable. The color sits in a soft clay. Mango quartz has no entry of its own in our Care Checker, so the ratings below start from [clear quartz](/stones/clear-quartz/) and add cautions for the clay.

- **Water.** OK for a quick wash, as for quartz.
- **Soaking.** Brief only. Care guidance for quartz covers washing, not a long soak.
- **Sun.** Unknown, so skip the sunny windowsill, the cautious choice.
- **Heat.** Unknown for the clay. Quartz is very resistant to heat, but the clay is unrated, so keep it away from heaters and flames, the cautious choice.
- **Chemicals.** Unknown for the clay, though quartz itself resists most chemicals. Skip household cleaners.
- **Salt.** Unknown, so skip salt.
- **Cleaning machines.** Skip ultrasonic and steam cleaners, the cautious choice for a stone with inclusions.
- **Storage.** OK for quartz. Wrap each point on its own so the tips do not knock together.
- **Toxicity.** OK to handle as quartz. Wearing and handling intact quartz carry negligible risk, while cutting and grinding dust is a silica hazard.

### How to Clean Mango Quartz

1. Dust it with a soft, dry brush, working from the base toward the tip.
2. If it is grimy, rinse it briefly in lukewarm water with one drop of mild soap, brushing the tips gently rather than scrubbing.
3. Pat it dry and let it air dry before you put it away.

### Can Mango Quartz Go in Water or Sun?

A quick wash, yes. A long soak, no, since our care data for quartz covers washing only. Sun is Unknown, so skip the sunny windowsill. Keep every stone out of water you drink, and check clear quartz in the [Care Checker](/tools/crystal-care-checker/) for the quartz side.

## Price Guide

We have no price data for mango quartz yet, so here is what moves the price.

- **Color.** Sellers prize vibrant orange-yellow tips.
- **Clarity.** Sellers also highlight clear, clean crystals.
- **Form.** It is sold as single points, double-terminated crystals, clusters and sprays like the one GIA pictured, so compare like with like.
- **The one-locality claim.** Sellers stress that it comes from a single place. That may support the price today, but it is their claim, so judge the piece, not the story.
