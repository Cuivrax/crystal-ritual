## How to Read These Times

Every time above is New York time, Eastern Standard Time until March 14, 2027, then Eastern Daylight Time until November 7, 2027. For Chicago take away one hour, for Denver two and for Los Angeles three. The calendar panel at the top of the page does the conversion for your own zone.

Across the four mainland US time zones, every full moon of 2027 falls on the same date. In Alaska and Hawaii, the Sturgeon Moon falls late on August 16 instead. Further east the dates move the other way. In London, the Cold Moon of 2026 falls on December 24, the Strawberry Moon on June 19, the Corn Moon on September 16 and the Beaver Moon on November 14.

The times are computed with the method of Jean Meeus in *Astronomical Algorithms* (1998) and rounded to the nearest minute. They match US Naval Observatory times for every date in these tables except one. The September 15 full moon falls about half a minute after 7:03 PM, so it rounds to 7:04 PM here and reads 7:03 PM in the Naval Observatory list.

## The Full Moon Names

The names follow the list popularized by the Old Farmer's Almanac, one name for each month, taken from the month of the US Eastern date. Many read like a farm and forest calendar, with snow in February, strawberries in June, corn in September and cold in December. The almanac says many of the names come from Native American, Colonial American and other traditional North American sources, and that the Wolf Moon is thought to have English origins ([Almanac.com](https://www.almanac.com/full-moon-names)).

### Harvest Moon 2027

By the almanac rule, the Harvest Moon is the full moon closest to the September equinox, so it can fall in September or October. In 2027 it is the September 15 moon, about a week before the equinox, and you will see it listed as the Harvest Moon as often as the Corn Moon. The Hunter's Moon is the full moon after it, on Friday, October 15.

### Is There a Blue Moon in 2027?

No. A Blue Moon in the popular sense is a second full moon in one calendar month, and every month of 2027 has exactly one. The most recent Blue Moon was on May 31, 2026.

## Eclipses in 2027

NASA lists five eclipses for 2027, each on a new or full moon from the tables above. Times below are NASA's times of greatest eclipse, converted to New York time.

| Date | Eclipse | Greatest eclipse (New York time) | Moon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saturday, February 6 | Annular solar | 11:00 AM EST | New moon |
| Saturday, February 20 | Penumbral lunar | 6:13 PM EST | Snow Moon |
| Sunday, July 18 | Penumbral lunar | 12:03 PM EDT | Buck Moon |
| Monday, August 2 | Total solar | 6:07 AM EDT | New moon |
| Tuesday, August 17 | Penumbral lunar | 3:14 AM EDT | Sturgeon Moon |

A penumbral lunar eclipse is the faint kind. The moon passes only through the outer part of Earth's shadow and dims a little, which is easy to miss. The July 18 eclipse peaks around midday in New York, when the full moon sits below the horizon there. To see where each eclipse is visible, check the maps on [NASA's 2027 eclipse page](https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/OH/OH2027.html).
