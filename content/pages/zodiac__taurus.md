# Taurus Crystals & Birthstones, Plus Where the Links Come From

Taurus has no single official birthstone, because birthstones belong to months and Taurus runs from about April 20 to May 20. That gives diamond for an April birthday and emerald for a May one. As a zodiac stone, a 1913 list gives Taurus the sapphire, while today's crystal guides favor blue kyanite and soft pink stones like rhodonite, morganite and rose quartz, all beliefs rather than mineral facts.

## What Is the Taurus Birthstone?

The honest answer has two parts, the month stone and the sign stone.

**The month stone.** On the modern list of the [American Gem Society](https://www.americangemsociety.org/birthstones/), April's birthstone is diamond and May's is emerald. Each was already the only stone for its month on the 1912 jewelers' list (Kunz 1913, page 319). Our [April](/birthstones/april/) and [May](/birthstones/may/) pages go stone by stone.

**The sign stone.** **Tradition.** In the verse list of zodiac gems that George Frederick Kunz printed in 1913, Taurus gets the sapphire. "If on your hand this stone you bind, / You in Taurus born will find," the verse begins, and it ends with a promise that "'twill cure diseases of the mind" (*The Curious Lore of Precious Stones*, page 346). No stone can keep that promise, so read it as a rhyme of its time. An old Spanish list that Kunz thought probably reflected Arab tradition gives Taurus ruby and diamond instead (page 347).

The sapphire has more than a verse behind it. Kunz called it "properly the gem for April" and objected when the 1912 list moved it to September (page 320). His calendar of the months gives April "diamond, sapphire," with Taurus as its sign and the daisy as its flower (page 328), and his list of American gems for each month gives April the sapphire of Montana and Idaho (page 323).

## What Color Is the Taurus Birthstone?

Taurus has two month colors, the diamond of April, best known colorless, and the green of emerald in May. The 1913 sapphire adds blue, its best-known color, though sapphire comes in every color but red. Modern guides add pink, from rhodonite, morganite, kunzite and rose quartz, plus the light to dark blue of blue kyanite.

## Taurus Crystals in Today's Guides

**Modern Association.** Guides describe Taurus as "an earth sign ruled by Venus, the planet of love," in one guide's words, and Venus explains the pinks. "Rose quartz is most commonly associated with Taurus and Libra, both of which are ruled by Venus," another explains. Guides also call the sign steady and sometimes stubborn, and some pick stones to soften that. One says morganite works on Taureans "by softening the edges that sometimes can be a little too hard," and a jewelry guide credits blue kyanite with help "during periods of stubborn stagnation." Here are the usual crystals for Taurus, with what each stone really is.

### Blue Kyanite

**Classic.** [Blue kyanite](/stones/blue-kyanite/) is an aluminum silicate mineral colored blue by iron and titanium. Its hardness changes with direction, from about 4.5 to 7 on the Mohs scale, which earned it the old name disthene, meaning two strengths. Guides usually name it for [Aries](/zodiac/aries/), Taurus and Libra together, and Taurus gets it most. Blue kyanite has also long been used to imitate sapphire, a small accident of history for a sign whose 1913 stone is the sapphire.

### Rhodonite

**Widely Linked.** [Rhodonite](/stones/rhodonite/) is a pink manganese silicate mineral, often veined with black manganese oxides, rated 5.5 to 6.5 and usually untreated. It is easily confused with rhodochrosite, which is softer and fizzes in hydrochloric acid ([rhodonite vs rhodochrosite](/compare/rhodonite-vs-rhodochrosite/)). One German guide calls rhodonite "the main stone for Taurus."

### Morganite & Kunzite

**Widely Linked.** [Morganite](/stones/morganite/) is the pink to orange-pink variety of beryl, the same mineral as emerald, colored by traces of manganese and rated 7.5 to 8. Light stones are often heated or irradiated to deepen the pink.

[Kunzite](/stones/kunzite/) is a pastel pink to violet variety of spodumene, also colored by manganese, and its color can fade in sunlight. It is named after George Frederick Kunz, and it was first recognized as a distinct variety of spodumene around 1902. The man who printed the Taurus sapphire verse in 1913 lent his name to one of the sign's modern stones.

### Rose Quartz

**Widely Linked.** [Rose quartz](/stones/rose-quartz/) is the pale to medium pink variety of quartz, rated 7 and usually untreated. Despite its reputation, ordinary rose quartz is generally stable in light and heat, which makes it the easiest Taurus pink to live with.

### Emerald for May Taurus

**Widely Linked.** [Emerald](/stones/emerald/) is the bluish green to green variety of beryl, colored by chromium, vanadium or both, and rated 7.5 to 8. Most cut emeralds are treated with oils, waxes or polymers to hide fractures, and the stone can be fragile despite its hardness. It is May's birthstone. It also has a solid modern Taurus link, and one guide even calls it "the birthstone for Taurus," which mixes up the month list and the sign list.

### Malachite & the Other Greens

**Often Linked.** Jade, agate, [malachite](/stones/malachite/), iolite and chrysoprase each turn up in a fair share of Taurus lists. Malachite is the touchy one, a soft green copper mineral at 3.5 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale whose grinding dust is toxic to breathe. One guide calls it a zodiac stone "for those born in the heart of spring."

### Diamond & Sapphire

**Rarely Linked.** Few modern guides tie [diamond](/stones/diamond/) or blue [sapphire](/stones/sapphire/) to Taurus, though a few give the sign pink or white sapphire. Diamond is pure carbon, 10 on the Mohs scale. Sapphire is gem corundum in any color but red, rated 9, and many sapphires on the market today are lab-grown.

## Where the Taurus Links Come From

**The Story.** Most of the old Taurus gem links run through one planet, Venus.

- **Medieval Jewish tradition.** **Tradition.** The tribe of Issachar was paired with Taurus (Kunz 1913, page 314).
- **Old color lore.** **Tradition.** Friday and Venus were represented by blue, "and the celestial-hued sapphire was the stone in which this color appeared in all its beauty" (page 31).
- **Old print.** **Tradition.** An old print of the Roman day gods pairs Venus with Taurus and the emerald, for Friday (page 332).
- **Old planetary lists.** **Tradition.** Venus received the sapphire, the carbuncle, coral and pearl, and "usually the emerald is the stone of Venus." In the Lapidario of Alfonso X, the diamond belonged "to the first degree of the sign Taurus" (page 348).
- **Old talisman lists.** **Tradition.** A list of constellation figures for engraved gems puts Andromeda under Taurus, of the nature of Venus, and says it "reconciles husband and wife, strengthens love" (page 353).
- **1913.** **Tradition.** Kunz's verse list names the sapphire (page 346), and the old Spanish list ruby and diamond (page 347).
- **Today.** **Modern Association.** Blue kyanite leads, followed by the Venus pinks and the May emerald.

So the 1913 sapphire and the May emerald are both old Venus stones, and today's pinks lean on Venus too. That thread is our reading, but every link in it comes from the lists above.

## How to Use Taurus Stones in Everyday Life

**The Ritual.** Taurus season falls in the middle of spring, when the evenings get longer. Whatever you believe about the stones, these give a pause you chose and something in your hand to mark it.

### A Slow Friday Dinner

**Try This.** The old print gives Friday to Venus and Taurus. On Friday evening, set a rose quartz on the table, put your phone in another room and eat one meal without hurrying. The stone marks the table, the dinner does the rest.

### A Small Change Stone

**Try This.** Guides call Taurus steady to the point of stubborn. Keep a rhodonite or a morganite on your planner. Each Monday, write one small thing you will do differently that week, a new route, a new recipe or a new way to answer an old argument, and set the stone on that line.

### A Birthday Card with the Old Stone

**Try This.** For an April Taurus, press a daisy, Kunz's April flower, into the card and copy the first two lines of the 1913 sapphire verse. For a May Taurus, an emerald covers the birthday and the old Venus link at once. Add a note that emeralds are fragile despite their hardness, so it is a ring for gentle days.

**Safety.** Malachite never goes in water you drink, and neither does any other stone. Small tumbled stones and bead bracelets are not toys. Keep them away from small children and pets who might swallow them. These are personal practices, not treatments.
