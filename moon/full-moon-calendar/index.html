Full Moon Calendar 2027 with Dates, Times & Names

There are 12 full moons in 2027, from the Wolf Moon on Friday, January 22, to the Cold Moon on Monday, December 13. No month holds two, so 2027 has no Blue Moon by the usual monthly rule. Below is every full moon and new moon from December 2026 to December 2027 with its traditional name and New York time, computed and rounded to the minute.

How to Read These Times

Every time above is New York time, Eastern Standard Time until March 14, 2027, then Eastern Daylight Time until November 7, 2027. For Chicago take away one hour, for Denver two and for Los Angeles three. The calendar panel at the top of the page does the conversion for your own zone.

Across the four mainland US time zones, every full moon of 2027 falls on the same date. In Alaska and Hawaii, the Sturgeon Moon falls late on August 16 instead. Further east the dates move the other way. In London, the Cold Moon of 2026 falls on December 24, the Strawberry Moon on June 19, the Corn Moon on September 16 and the Beaver Moon on November 14.

The times are computed with the method of Jean Meeus in Astronomical Algorithms (1998) and rounded to the nearest minute. They match the US Naval Observatory list for every date in these tables except one. The September 15 full moon falls about half a minute after 7:03 PM, so it rounds to 7:04 PM here and reads 7:03 PM in that list.

The Full Moon Names

The names follow the list popularized by the Old Farmer's Almanac, one name for each month, taken from the month of the US Eastern date. Many read like a farm and forest calendar, with snow in February, strawberries in June, corn in September and cold in December. The almanac says many of the names come from Native American, Colonial American and other traditional North American sources, and that the Wolf Moon is thought to have English origins.

Harvest Moon 2027

By the almanac rule, the Harvest Moon is the full moon closest to the September equinox, so it can fall in September or October. In 2027 it is the September 15 moon, about a week before the equinox, and you will see it listed as the Harvest Moon as often as the Corn Moon. The Hunter's Moon is the full moon after it, on Friday, October 15.

Is There a Blue Moon in 2027?

No. A Blue Moon in the popular sense is a second full moon in one calendar month, and every month of 2027 has exactly one. The most recent Blue Moon was on May 31, 2026.

Eclipses in 2027

There are five eclipses in 2027, each on a new or full moon from the tables above. Times below are the times of greatest eclipse, converted to New York time.

Date Eclipse Greatest eclipse (New York time) Moon
Saturday, February 6 Annular solar 11:00 AM EST New moon
Saturday, February 20 Penumbral lunar 6:13 PM EST Snow Moon
Sunday, July 18 Penumbral lunar 12:03 PM EDT Buck Moon
Monday, August 2 Total solar 6:07 AM EDT New moon
Tuesday, August 17 Penumbral lunar 3:14 AM EDT Sturgeon Moon

A penumbral lunar eclipse is the faint kind. The moon passes only through the outer part of Earth's shadow and dims a little, which is easy to miss. The July 18 eclipse peaks around midday in New York, when the full moon sits below the horizon there. To see where each eclipse is visible, check the maps on NASA's 2027 eclipse page.

A Stone for Each Full Moon?

Crystal guides do not agree on a stone for each named moon, and no old gem lore text we found assigns one. The stone they tie to the full moon most consistently is moonstone, rated Classic on our scale, with the story behind it on our moon page. If you want one stone per month, the birthstone list is the one with a documented history, and it is on our birthstones by month page.
